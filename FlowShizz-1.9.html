FlowShizz 1.9

One trial, both devices

  • Start the 14-day trial wherever you like. Tap it on your Mac and your iPhone shows the same days remaining, or start it on your iPhone and this Mac picks it up. Previously each one wanted its own, and neither countdown ever began — they were each waiting for the other.
  • The days now start when you tap, so you always know where you stand.
  • If your other device hasn't turned up yet, FlowShizz says so while there's still time to do something about it — no quietly spending a fortnight trying something you never got to see.

Blocking is free and always will be. Pro only covers blocking your iPhone and your Mac together — bought once, on the iPhone, and yours for life.
