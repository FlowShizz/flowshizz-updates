FlowShizz 1.4

Updating actually restarts now

  • "Install and Relaunch" didn't relaunch. Keeping FlowShizz alive in the menu bar meant refusing to quit — and an update needs the app to quit so it can be replaced. So the new version was installed while the old one kept running, and FlowShizz went on reporting the old version until you quit it by hand from the menu bar. It now steps aside for its own updates and comes back on its own.

One last time: this update is delivered by the version that has the bug, so it may still need a manual quit and relaunch to take effect. Updates after this one will just work.
