FlowShizz 1.17

Fixes

  • A pomodoro block running on both devices now blocks the same list on each. The Mac was using its Pomodoro Block list and the iPhone its Quick Block one, so once you'd edited either, the two stopped agreeing.
  • The Pomodoro Block tab now shows and edits its own list. It was displaying the Quick Block list while saving your changes to the pomodoro one — every edit worked and none of it was visible.

Blocking is free and always will be. Pro covers blocking your iPhone and your Mac together, and Pomodoro Block.
