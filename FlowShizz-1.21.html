FlowShizz 1.21

New

  • FlowShizz opens on a home screen of cards — Quick Block, Schedules & Presets, Pomodoro Block — instead of a row of tabs. Every screen has a Home button back to it.
  • Pomodoro Block now chimes and posts a notification at every break and every return to work. It used to change phase in silence: your apps unblocked and re-blocked and the app never said a word.
  • The menu bar shows which stretch you're on and how long is left of it, and Scheduler Status is one click away from there — including while a block is running, which is when you most want to see what's armed next.

Changed

  • During a pomodoro block the big number is now the phase — "24:56 to the break" — with the whole session on a line beneath it. It was the other way round, and the bigger number was the one that mattered least.
  • The stretches you've finished fill in warm, matching the progress bar, and a break is the one cool segment. You can tell whether you're working without reading a word.
  • The cycles picker says what you're actually choosing: "2 cycles · 8 × 25 min · 4h 20m". "2 cycles" means nothing until you know that the total includes the breaks.
  • Pomodoro Block no longer shows Quick Block's hours-and-minutes card or its Start button. They set a length the mode ignores.

Fixed

  • When a schedule overlapped a pomodoro, "whole session ends" showed the end of everything rather than the end of the pomodoro, so the session looked longer than it was.
  • The end-of-block notification uses the app's own chime instead of the system alert sound.

Blocking is free and always will be. Pro covers blocking your iPhone and your Mac together, and Pomodoro Block.
