FlowShizz 1.3

Blocks from your iPhone arrive properly now

  • Up to a minute late, now a few seconds. FlowShizz was only checking for a block started on your iPhone once a minute unless it happened to be the app you were looking at — which, sitting in the menu bar while you work, it almost never is. That left a gap where your Mac was still open. It now checks every ten seconds regardless.

It tells you the truth about syncing

  • When there's nothing to sync with yet, it says Waiting for your other device rather than reporting a successful sync that never happened.

Blocking is free and always will be. Pro only covers blocking your iPhone and your Mac together.
