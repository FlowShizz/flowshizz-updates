FlowShizz 1.8

Your Mac now knows when you've bought Pro

  • If you bought FlowShizz Pro on your iPhone, this Mac will now recognise it. Until now it couldn't: checking whether you owned Pro was itself treated as a Pro feature, so a Mac that hadn't been given Pro could never find out it had been. It greeted people who had already paid by offering them a free trial.
  • FlowShizz now checks what you own as soon as it starts, before deciding what it's allowed to do. Nothing else is read unless you have Pro — your lists, schedules and blocks are still private to you and still need Pro to travel between devices.

The iPhone app is now on the App Store. Blocking is free on both. Pro only covers blocking your iPhone and your Mac together — one payment, for life.
