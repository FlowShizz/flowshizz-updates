FlowShizz 1.7

A steadier check on website blocking

  • 1.6 renewed the permission that lets FlowShizz block websites every time it updated. That turned out to be both unnecessary and risky — a working setup survives an update perfectly well, and renewing it needlessly could leave you with none at all.
  • FlowShizz now simply checks whether it works when it starts, and only repairs the permission if it doesn't answer. A setup that's fine is left alone.
  • If it can't be repaired, FlowShizz tells you and asks for your password when a block starts, so blocks are always enforced — and it tries once, rather than fiddling with the setting every time you open it.

Blocking is free and always will be. Pro only covers blocking your iPhone and your Mac together.
