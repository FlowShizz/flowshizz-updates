FlowShizz 1.11

Drop in an app, not just a website

  • You can now add an app to a block that's already running, the same way you could already add a website. Pick it, and it's shut for the rest of the block.
  • It's still one-way. Nothing can be taken out until the block ends, and dropped-in items leave when it does.

You can actually find it now

  • Drop-in used to be a line of small text at the very bottom of the window. It's now a panel on the block screen — "Something else distracting you?" — because it's the only thing you can do while a block runs, and it was hiding.

Blocking is free and always will be. Pro only covers blocking your iPhone and your Mac together.
