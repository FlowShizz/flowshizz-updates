FlowShizz 1.13

A drop-in stays with its own block

  • Something you added to one block could come back in a later one, if you'd quit the app in between. Dropped-in apps and websites now end with the block they were added to and never return on their own.
  • They were never added to your saved lists — nothing you dropped in has been quietly kept.

Blocking is free and always will be. Pro only covers blocking your iPhone and your Mac together.
