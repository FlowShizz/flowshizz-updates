FlowShizz 1.5

Important: website blocking could stop working after an update

  • Please install this one. Updating FlowShizz could quietly break the part of it that blocks websites. Apps still got blocked and the countdown still ran, so there was nothing to notice — but websites were left open.
  • The cause: the background component that edits your system's website list needs permission tied to the exact copy of the app that asked for it, and replacing the app during an update left that permission pointing at the old copy. FlowShizz now renews it whenever it updates, and checks that the component actually answers.
  • If it ever can't, FlowShizz now says so plainly and asks for your password instead, so a block is never left unenforced. It will no longer tell you a block is running when part of it isn't.

Blocking is free and always will be. Pro only covers blocking your iPhone and your Mac together.
