FlowShizz 1.14

Pomodoro Block

  • A block that enforces the breaks as well as the focus. Twenty-five minutes of work, five off, four rounds to a cycle, and a long break between cycles. The break ends whether you like it or not — which is the part every other timer leaves to your good intentions.
  • Measured in cycles rather than minutes, so a block always ends on work and never hands you back time you asked to lose. One cycle is 1h 55m.
  • It has its own list, which starts as a copy of your Quick Block list. Change either and they go their separate ways.

Also

  • The pomodoro finally makes a sound. It has called for chimes since the first release and the files were never there — so it has been silent all along.
  • Editing a preset now shows the other device's apps, and lets you remove them.

Blocking is free and always will be. Pro only covers blocking your iPhone and your Mac together.
