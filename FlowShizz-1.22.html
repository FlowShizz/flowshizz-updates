FlowShizz 1.22

Fixes

  • FlowShizz now asks to send notifications when it opens. It only asked the first time it had something to say, which meant it never appeared in System Settings › Notifications at all — and the notification that triggered the prompt was dropped, so the first one was always the one you missed.
  • Notifications now appear while FlowShizz is the app you're looking at. Anyone watching the countdown got the chime and no banner.
  • "Start a Pomodoro timer" is no longer offered during a Pomodoro Block. It would have started a second, unenforced timer running its own phases alongside the real one.

New

  • When a schedule overlaps a Pomodoro Block and outlives it, FlowShizz says so: "Pomodoro finished — your schedule keeps the block running until 16:30. No more breaks." Previously the breaks simply stopped coming and nothing was said.

Blocking is free and always will be. Pro covers blocking your iPhone and your Mac together, and Pomodoro Block.
