FlowShizz 1.1

The menu bar does the work now

  • It tells you what's going on. What's blocking and for how long, which preset it belongs to, when the next scheduled window starts, and when your devices last synced — without opening the app.
  • Closing FlowShizz no longer stops it. ⌘Q now tucks the app away into the menu bar and keeps it working, so scheduled blocks still start and blocks begun on your iPhone still arrive. Quitting properly is in the menu-bar item, and it says what it will cost before you do it.
  • Sync now is there when you need it, for Pro.

Looks

  • Bigger, glossier controls with room to breathe — it was wearing an iPhone layout on a desktop screen.
  • A warmer accent borrowed from flowshizz.com, used for what's running: the whole screen shifts colour while a block is on.

Blocking is free and always will be. Pro only covers syncing with the iPhone app.
