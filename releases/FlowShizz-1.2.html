FlowShizz 1.2

The iPhone app is here

  • FlowShizz for iPhone is on the App Store. With Pro, one tap starts the same block on both machines — and this update is what makes that dependable, so it is worth installing before you pair them up.

Blocks no longer go missing

  • A running block could vanish. If one device was relaunched while a shared block was still going, it could come back believing nothing was running and wipe the block for the other device too. Both halves are fixed: the block now survives a restart, and no device can overwrite something it hasn't seen yet.

Fixes

  • FlowShizz no longer quits unexpectedly when your Mac starts up.

Blocking is free and always will be. Pro only covers blocking your iPhone and your Mac together.
