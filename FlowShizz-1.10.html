FlowShizz 1.10

Different hours on different days

  • Schedules used to apply the same hours to every day you picked. Now each day has its own — 9 to 5 Monday to Thursday, 9 to 1 on Friday, whatever suits.
  • Tap a day to switch it on; it starts with the hours of the day you were last editing, so the usual case stays quick. Days that differ from the rest are marked, so nothing is hiding.
  • Your existing schedules carry over exactly as they were, now editable per day.

Add to a block that's already running

  • Thought of something else after you'd started? You can now add a website to a block in progress. You still can't take anything out — a block only ever gets stronger.

Fixes

  • Editing a preset on one device no longer wipes the other's apps from it. Your Mac can't see iPhone apps and your iPhone can't see Mac apps, and whichever you saved last was quietly publishing the other's list as empty.

Blocking is free and always will be. Pro only covers blocking your iPhone and your Mac together.
